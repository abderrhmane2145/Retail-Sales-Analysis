
# Retail Sales Analysis Project

## Problem Statement
**Main Question:**  
How do customer demographics and shopping habits influence retail sales, and what insights can guide product and pricing strategies?

---

## Sub‑Questions
1. Does age and gender affect purchasing behavior?  
2. Which product categories are most popular?  
3. Are there seasonal shopping trends?  
4. How does spending vary across age groups and product preferences?  
5. What insights can be drawn from product price distributions?

---

## Analysis Types
- **Descriptive Analysis**  
  Summarize customer demographics, product categories, and overall sales trends.  

- **Diagnostic Analysis**  
  Investigate shopping habits and seasonal variations to understand *why* certain patterns occur.  

- **Prescriptive Analysis**  
  Provide actionable recommendations for product and pricing strategies based on insights.  

---

## Project Objectives
- Explore demographic influences on purchasing behavior.  
- Identify high‑performing product categories.  
- Detect seasonal or temporal sales trends.  
- Analyze spending patterns across age groups and preferences.  
- Examine product price distributions for strategic pricing.   

---

## Deliverables
- Clean dataset with documented transformations.  
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) report with visuals.    
- Recommendations for product and pricing strategies.  
- Visualization dashboard or summary charts.  

---

## Scope
- **Dataset:** Retail Sales and Customer Demographics ([Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/mohammadtalib786/retail-sales-dataset)).  
- **Tools:** Excel, SQL Server, Python (Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn).  
- **Focus:** Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), visualization, and actionable insights.  

---

## Stakeholders
- **Retail Managers:** Interested in customer insights for operational decisions.  
- **Marketing Teams:** Use demographic and behavioral insights for targeted campaigns.  
- **Product Teams:** Apply findings to pricing and category strategies.
