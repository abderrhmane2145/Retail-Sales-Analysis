# Actionable Insights - Retail Sales Analysis

## Key Findings Summary

### 1. Summary Statistics & Distributions
- **Revenue**: Strongly right-skewed (Mean 456 >> Median 135) → Driven by a small number of big spenders. Risk if we lose them.
- **Unit Price**: Right-skewed → Most sales are low-price items.
- **Quantity**: Stable and uniform (Mean ≈ Median).
- **Age**: Uniform distribution → Customers from all age segments.

### 2. Correlations
- **Very Strong**: Unit Price ↔ Revenue (0.8–1.0) → Expensive items drive most revenue.
- **Weak**: Quantity ↔ Revenue (0.2–0.39) → Selling many cheap items helps but does not dominate.
- **Very Weak/Negligible**: Age has almost no impact on Revenue, Quantity, or Unit Price.

**Conclusion**: Upselling has much higher impact than cross-selling.

### 3. Revenue by Dimensions
- **Date**: Clear peak and low months exist.
- **Category**:
  - Electronics: High unit price → Ideal for upselling.
  - Clothing: High quantity sold → Ideal for cross-selling.
- **Gender**: No significant difference → No high impact on business.
- **Age Groups**: Highest revenue contribution from 26–50.

### 4. Customer Analysis
- **Gender**: Keep marketing strategy normal (no need for gender-specific campaigns).
- **Category**:
  - Clothing → Cross-selling + slight price increase.
  - Electronics → Upselling.
- **Age Groups**:
  - Prioritize 26–50 for cross-selling.
  - 18–25 show highest efficiency for upselling.

### 5. Boxplots
- No significant outliers → Business behavior is predictable and stable.

## Actionable Recommendations

- **Retention Program** for big spenders (protect key revenue source).
- **Upselling** focused on:
  - Electronics category
  - Age 18–25
  - Peak months
- **Cross-selling** focused on:
  - Clothing category
  - Age 26–50
  - Low-revenue months
- **Slightly raise prices** on premium items (supported by strong price-revenue link).
- **Marketing**:
  - Keep gender-neutral (normal strategy).
  - Extra focus on age 18–25.
- **Maintain balance** between upselling (higher impact) and cross-selling (volume support).

These actions will help stabilize revenue, protect high-value customers, and maximize growth through targeted upselling while using cross-selling to boost volume in the right segments.
